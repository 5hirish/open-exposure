# Pilot plan: 6 weeks to a go, pivot or stop decision

The pilot covers 40 products: about 25 high-demand AI apps for search traffic and 15 browser
extensions for impact. It answers one question: can one person produce verified reports
cheaply enough that someone cares? Targets live in [`targets.yaml`](targets.yaml).

| Phase | Weeks | Work | Gate at the end |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Setup | 1 | Lawyer reviews charter, lock the 40-product list, finalize canary session | Gate 1: lawyer clears charter |
| Analyze | 2 to 3 | Trace and capture all 40, human-verify each, log hours per report, send drafts | Gate 2: 2 h or less per report, lawyer clears first 3 |
| Right of reply | 4 to 5 | 14-day reply window, apply corrections, build static site, line up press | |
| Publish and pitch | 6 | Publish, pitch press, OCU and BEUC, measure response | Decision: go, pivot or stop |

Nothing publishes before Gate 1.

## Kill criteria at week 6

| Signal | Target | If missed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hours per verified report | 2 h or less | Above 6 h: stop, or recruit reviewers first |
| Reports published | 30 or more of 40 | Under 15: rework the pipeline before more products |
| Products with at least 1 mismatch | 1 in 5 or more | None: switch category |
| Press or consumer group pickup | 1 or more | None, and no developer interest: stop |
| Developers asking for review or a badge | 3 or more | Below 3: stay watchdog-only, no CLI or badge layer |
| Credible legal threats | 0 | Any: pause publishing, review with counsel |
